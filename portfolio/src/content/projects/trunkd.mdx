This is a project to ingest public safety communications within the Washington DC area, transcribe them in real-time,
and use an AI agent to categorize and manage a real-time incident board for interested parties to keep updated on public safety
concerns as they happen.

A Whisper model performs the transcription, with an additional workflow for evaluating different transcription models or settings.
I additionally use entity extraction combined with DC street names and other vocabulary to improve signal, provide contextual links,
and allow for geofenced notifications. The incident board management agent has access to tools allowing it to research transmissions and use audio-capable reasoning model
to get a second opinion on transcripts or verify its understanding.

Using NATS JetStream and an optimized SDR -> WebSockets path I'm able to measurably play transcoded audio samples in the browser less than a second after RF capture,
with transcripts usually following within a few seconds (and often competing).

The system also provides a performant and expressive search mechanism and an archive of transmissions going back roughly a year.
